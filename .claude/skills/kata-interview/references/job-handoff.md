# Job handoff: two Asks

Templates and worked examples for the two `Ask` calls in
`SKILL.md` § Step 5.

## Ask 1 — Introduction

Keep Ask 1 short and uniform across every interview. Phrase it like a
human interviewer who opens a conversation. Do **not** mention
`CLAUDE.md`. The harness loads it automatically.

> Hi, thanks for your time. Before we get into it, tell me a bit about
> yourself: who you are, your role and team, and what you have on your
> plate lately.

## Ask 2 — Job Delivery
