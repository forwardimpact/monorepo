# Product brief — `todo` CLI

`todo` is a minimal, local, single-user command-line task tracker. It offers
`add`, `list`, and `done`. A local JSON store holds the data. It is deliberately
small. It stays fast and dependency-free.

**In scope:** local task capture and retrieval that keeps the CLI simple and
quick. This covers flags and output that help one person manage their own list.

**Out of scope:** multi-user or social features, networked sync, accounts, and
visual themes or animation. These contradict the "minimal local single-user
CLI" direction. They are not planned.
